Privacy Policy — GSB Field Ops

Last updated: September 2026

GSB Field Ops is an internal operations app built for and used exclusively by employees and authorized team members of GSB General Construction Inc. This app is not available to the general public and is not intended for use by anyone outside of GSB General Construction Inc's team.

What We Collect

To operate this app, we collect and store the following information, entered directly by our team members:

  • Client information: name, phone number, email address, and property address, for the purpose of managing construction projects and contracts.
  • Photos: job site photos, before/after project photos, and floor plan sketches, taken and uploaded by our team during the course of a project.
  • Signatures: digital signatures captured for contract and estimate approval.
  • Team member login information: name and an internal access code used to identify which team member is using the app.

How We Use This Information

All information collected is used solely for the operation of GSB General Construction Inc's business — managing client relationships, on-site estimates, contracts, project tracking, and internal scheduling. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Where This Data Is Stored

App data is stored securely using Google Firebase, a cloud database and hosting service. Access to this data is restricted to authorized GSB General Construction Inc team members.

Data Retention

Client, project, and contract information is retained for as long as necessary to support ongoing business operations, recordkeeping, and legal obligations related to construction contracts and warranties.
